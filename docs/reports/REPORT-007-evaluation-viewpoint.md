# REPORT-007 — Model Evaluation and Viewpoint Generalisation Experiment

**Date:** 2026-11-19 **Status:** Complete

## A. Task summary

- **Requested:** real precision, recall, mAP50 and mAP50-95, with per-class results and a confusion matrix. Also a viewpoint experiment: train mainly on frontal images, test on held-out side and overhead images, report the real mAP50-95 difference, and keep it distinct from ordinary validation.
- **Done:** standard validation of the app model, plus a full viewpoint experiment with a separate frontal-only model, confound controls, and a second-model comparison.
- **Completion:** full, with documented limitations.

## B. Technical implementation

- `src/evaluation/evaluate.py` runs `model.val()` on the official val split and saves `metrics.json` with overall and per-class metrics, speed, and plots (confusion matrix, PR, F1, P and R curves).
- `src/evaluation/viewpoint_evaluation.py`:
  - `prepare`: builds the groups from `data/viewpoint_metadata.csv` and enforces a leakage check, then writes image lists and YAMLs.
  - `evaluate`: runs `model.val()` per group and saves per-group and per-class metrics, the "frontal − group" mAP50-95 differences and plots per model.
  - `--val-only`: restricts evaluation to val-split groups. This is required for the main model, which was trained on the whole train split.
  - `--name` / `--description`: label the results.
- `scripts/run_viewpoint_experiment.sh` runs all steps (prepare, train frontal-only, evaluate both models).
- The dashboard's *Model Evaluation* page shows both evaluations in separate sections: KPIs with deltas, a group chart, tables and a two-model comparison chart.

## G. Model and dataset status

### Standard validation (app model `models/trained/best.pt`, val split 200 images)

P 0.832, R 0.681, mAP50 0.734, **mAP50-95 0.523**. Weakest class: `no_goggle` (mAP50 0.148).

### Viewpoint experiment (frontal-only model, trained on 527 frontal images)

| Group (never trained on) | Images | mAP50-95 | Frontal − group |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frontal (val) | 112 | 0.639 | — |
| Side | 116 | 0.678 | −0.040 |
| Overhead | 219 | 0.185 | +0.454 |
| Low angle | 13 | 0.185 | +0.454 (too few) |
| All non-frontal | 348 | 0.297 | +0.342 |

Controls:

- Close-ups only: frontal 0.673 vs side 0.699.
- Wide scenes only: frontal 0.341 (n = 7) vs overhead 0.177.
- On the same val images, the main model (trained on all viewpoints) improves overhead from 0.227 to 0.309.

**Conclusion:** the model generalises to side views at eye level but not to overhead / elevated CCTV-style views. Part of the overhead drop comes from smaller objects and a different image style (a confound that cannot be fully separated with 7 frontal wide-scene images). Training on all viewpoints narrows the gap but does not close it.
